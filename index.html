Off With My Head 圓圓劍仙

Our first title. A pick-up-and-play action game set in a world of sword sects and cultivation.

Fight through chapters of enemy hordes and bosses, build a different set of sword skills every run, and grow your swordsman between runs with gear, mounts and cultivation realms. Join a clan to progress together with other players.
